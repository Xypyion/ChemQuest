# Anti-slop audit 001 (2026-11-16)

Scope: the student pages of StoiVenture on `feat/unslop`. Rules: anti-slop 3.2.30. The owner asked for the audit and the fixes in one go, so each finding below already has its outcome.

Contrast figures are WCAG 2.x ratios, computed, not judged by eye. The pass mark is 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.

## Kept on purpose

- **It looks like a Valorant menu (R-30).** The owner asked for exactly that.
- **Capitals in the menu and titles (R-06).** Part of the same brief.
- **Green, yellow and grey on levels; gold, silver and bronze on badges (R-29).** They are states and tiers, and each also has an icon or a label.
- **Emoji on levels.** The teacher picks them. They are content.
- **The Lab's dark theme and the teacher console.** Not part of this pass.

## Not done

- **Em dashes in the older text** (finding 10), waiting on the owner.
- **Tablet widths (641 to 1279px):** the top bar still wraps to two rows with a strip that scrolls sideways. That behaviour is older than the skin.
- **Quests, battle and challenge pages** got the shape and contrast fixes only. Their layouts were not redesigned.
- **Not measured:** Lighthouse, Core Web Vitals, and real phones.
